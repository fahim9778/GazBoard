GazBoard is a whiteboard for teaching and thinking out loud. It works fully offline: no account, no sign-in, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. Your boards stay on your device.

<b>Write like on paper</b>
• Six pens, a pencil, a highlighter and a partial eraser. Ink stays exactly as you wrote it, with pressure from your stylus.
• The pen draws and your finger moves the board, both at once. On a Galaxy S Pen, hold the side button to erase.
• Hold Shift (with a keyboard) for a perfectly straight line.

<b>Paper for every subject</b>
• An infinite canvas, or a pad of A4, Letter, A3, A5 or Legal pages.
• Grid, dots, lines, columns, graph paper, notebook paper with the date, music staff and guitar tab.
• A page strip with a picture of every page, to jump straight to one.

<b>More than ink</b>
• Sticky notes, text with bold, italic and colour, shapes and arrows that snap to things, tables, emoji.
• Maths typed in LaTeX, drawn beautifully.
• Bring in PDF pages and pictures, then write on top of them.
• Link one board to another with @.

<b>Made for the classroom</b>
• Present mode, a laser pointer, answer covers to lift one at a time and a class timer.
• View mode: move around a finished board freely while nothing on it can change. The laser still points and the highlighter's marks fade away by themselves.

<b>Yours to keep</b>
• My boards, with coloured folders.
• Export to PNG, PDF or SVG, or save a copy as a file.
• Share a board with another GazBoard on the same Wi-Fi, device to device, with nothing going through the internet.
