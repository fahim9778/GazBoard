## What's new in GazBoard 6.0.0

**Record a lesson.** Press record (Ctrl+Alt+R, or ⌘⌥R on a Mac; also More → Record a lesson), teach as usual, press stop: your voice and the board become one sharp video, with the ink appearing as you wrote it and the view following your pans and zooms. Pause any time. The pen stays as smooth as ever, because nothing is filmed while you teach; the video is drawn afterwards, at up to 2560 pixels across. On computers and Android phones.

**Picture from phone.** Insert → Picture from phone shows a QR code. Scan it with any phone, take a photo, and it lands on the board a second later. Nothing to install on the phone, and nothing leaves your Wi-Fi.
