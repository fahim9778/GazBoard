## What's Changed

**New board inside a folder:** New board pressed while looking at a folder puts the new board in that folder. Before, the board you had open was moved there instead. Cancel now leaves My boards open, too.

**Nothing lost when you switch boards:** strokes, a rename or text you were typing just before opening another board or starting a new one are now saved first. Before, the last few seconds of work could be lost.

**Recent:** a board you make shows in Recent straight away. A blank board GazBoard puts up by itself joins Recent once you write on it or name it. After deleting a board inside a folder, the blank board that replaces it stays in that folder.

**Opening a file again:** if the board from a file has newer work on this computer, GazBoard now asks before opening the old file over it.

**Folders stay with your boards:** folders made in My boards are now saved beside the boards in a chosen boards folder, so they survive switching folders and show up on the phone's safe copy.

**Scrolling through pages:** on a board with pages (A4, A5 and so on) the mouse wheel now scrolls down through the pages, like Word or a PDF reader, and Ctrl+wheel zooms. Infinite boards keep the wheel for zooming, and "Mouse wheel zooms" in Settings keeps it zooming everywhere.

**Pencil:** undo after "Erase pencil marks" puts the pencil back under the ink, and locked pencil lines are mentioned instead of ignored. A pencil on its side can be selected and erased across all of its shading. Choosing the Pencil effect updates the pen menu straight away.

**Smaller fixes:** undoing a delete of several things restores their stacking order. Clicking the board already open in My boards no longer reloads it and wipes undo. The stylus nib shows light ink on a dark board. Board files with unusual ids can no longer write outside the boards folder.
