## What's Changed

**Recent boards:** My boards now opens with the boards you opened last, newest first, each with its picture, the folder it's filed in and when you opened it. Tap one to open it. A board that's gone, deleted or left behind in another boards folder, is marked Not available instead of opening, and its × takes it off the list. Each device keeps its own list.

**Search in My boards:** type in the box at the top and every board and folder that matches shows up as you type, wherever it's filed, with the matching words highlighted. A folder's name finds the boards inside it too. Enter opens the best match, Escape clears the search.
