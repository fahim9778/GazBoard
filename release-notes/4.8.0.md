## What's Changed

**Recent boards:** My boards now opens with the boards you opened last, newest first, each with its picture, the folder it's filed in and when you opened it. They're ordinary boards: tick, move, delete or drag them onto a folder right there. A board that's gone, deleted or left behind in another boards folder, is marked Not available instead of opening, and its × takes it off the list. Each device keeps its own list.

**Search in My boards:** type in the box at the top and every board and folder that matches shows up as you type, wherever it's filed, with the matching words highlighted. A folder's name finds the boards inside it too. Tick what it found to move or export it, or Select all to take every result. Enter opens the best match, Escape clears the search. Thanks to @Tousifrahmananto, whose pull request #39 brought selecting and moving search results.
