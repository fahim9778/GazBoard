## What's Changed

**Straight lines with Shift:** hold Shift while drawing with the pen, pencil or highlighter and you get a straight line, as in Microsoft Whiteboard. Close to level, upright or diagonal, it snaps exactly onto it, so music staves and guitar tabs come out truly parallel.

**Starts sooner:** the board you had open last is read while the window is still loading, and a big board is handed over in one go, so it appears sooner, especially from a boards folder in Google Drive or OneDrive.
