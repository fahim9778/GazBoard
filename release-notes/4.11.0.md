## What's Changed

**Triangle rulers:** tap the ⋯ on the ruler (or right-click it) and choose Triangle 45° or Triangle 30°/60°. Pens snap to all three edges, so right angles and 45°, 30° and 60° lines come out dead straight.

**Protractor:** the same menu now has a half-circle protractor with a mark for every degree and two scales, blue from the right and dark from the left. Draw along its straight edge for a line through the centre, along its curve for a perfect arc, or turn the red measuring arm by its knob to any whole degree and draw along it for a line at exactly that angle. Both angles the arm makes are shown beside the centre.

**0° can point up:** in the same menu, choose 0° points Up ↑ and the angle reading counts from straight up instead of from the right.

**See-through ruler:** the See-through slider makes the plastic lighter, so you can read the writing underneath. GazBoard remembers your shape, 0° direction and see-through level.

**Ruler fixes:** the straight ruler now shows its angle in the middle, and a line started right on a corner follows the side you draw along (or your hand, if you pull away from the ruler) instead of shooting off along the other side. Writing that only passes near a ruler, triangle or protractor is no longer pulled onto its edge: only a line started against the edge is held straight.
