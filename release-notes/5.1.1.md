## What's new in GazBoard 5.1.1

**Erasing shapes is now your choice.** The eraser leaves lines, arrows and shapes alone again, so a stray rub can't take a diagram with it. To have it rub out the shapes it crosses, turn on "Erase shapes too" in the eraser's menu (tap the eraser again). Ink inside a box, boxes with words in them and locked shapes are never erased.

**On Android, this update also brings everything from 5.1.0:** the neon pen, press and hold that picks up your drawing instead of opening the shortcut ring, lines that join where you draw them, arrows side by side, a fuller toolbar on a phone turned sideways, and ink that stops cleanly at the screen edge.
