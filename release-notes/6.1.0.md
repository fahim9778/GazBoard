## What's new in GazBoard 6.1.0

**Smoother writing on big boards.** Autosave now does its work in tiny pieces and lets your pen go first, so the small hitch you could feel while a full board was being saved is gone. Your boards are saved exactly as before.

**Long lessons, no stop.** A recording no longer stops at one hour. It keeps going, and when you press stop it comes out as part 1, part 2 and so on (an hour each, half an hour on a phone), saved together in one folder. Every video is named after the board and the time it began, so no two ever clash.
