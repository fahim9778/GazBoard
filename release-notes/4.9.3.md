## What's Changed

**Faster start with a boards folder in the cloud:** GazBoard no longer reads every board in your boards folder before its window appears. It also keeps a small index of your boards on this computer, so My boards opens quickly even when the folder is in Google Drive or OneDrive.

**A starting-up window:** on a slower computer, a small window with the GazBoard logo shows while it starts and tells you which board it is opening. The main window then opens straight onto your board.

**Smoother panning on busy boards:** moving around a board full of writing, especially zoomed out, no longer redraws every stroke on every frame. The picture slides along and only the part coming into view is drawn, so panning keeps up with your hand on slower laptops.
