## What's Changed

**The shortcut ring.** Hold your pen or finger still on empty board and a ring of shortcuts opens right where you are: your three latest pens, the highlighter, the eraser, the lasso, Paste and Insert, with Undo and Redo in the middle. Slide onto one and lift, or lift first and tap. Pens picked from the ring are the same pens as in the tray. A mouse right-click still opens the full list.

**Filling in small shapes with the pen no longer opens a menu.** Colouring in a note head or a small circle used to count as holding the pen still. GazBoard now tells a resting pen from a scribbling one, and Windows' own pen press-and-hold opens the ring instead of the old list. To keep a held pen from ever opening anything, turn off Settings → Hold the pen still for menus.

**Type the protractor's angle.** The protractor's ⋯ menu has an Arm angle box: type 37 and the arm points at exactly 37°.

**Small fixes from our Google Play testers.** On a phone, Present mode now says to tap ✕ on the bar instead of pressing Esc, and the backup buttons in My boards line up with the button above them.
