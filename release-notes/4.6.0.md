## What's Changed

**Update from inside GazBoard:** when a new version is out, GazBoard now offers to download it and restart into it, instead of only opening the download page. Your boards and settings stay exactly as they are, the download is checked against the release before anything changes, and you choose between restarting now or letting it go in when you close GazBoard. After the restart GazBoard tells you it is up to date, or, if the update could not go in, that nothing was changed and how to finish it.

**Windows downloads only what changed:** the installed Windows app fetches just the parts of the new version that are different, so most updates are much smaller than the full download. The portable .exe cannot replace itself and keeps opening the download page.

**Mac updates without the website:** the Mac app downloads the version for your chip, checks it, and swaps itself on restart, keeping the old app until the new one is safely in place. If macOS asks, allow GazBoard under System Settings, Privacy & Security, App Management. A Mac still running GazBoard from the disk image or from Downloads gets the download page instead, so move GazBoard into Applications first.

**Checks while GazBoard stays open:** a GazBoard left running all day now notices a new version without being restarted, still looking no more than twice a day, and never downloading anything until you say so. Saying Later means it will not ask again until GazBoard is next started.

**Start here:** this is the first version that can update itself, so this one is downloaded and installed the usual way. Every version after it can be installed from inside GazBoard.
