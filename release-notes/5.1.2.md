## What's new in GazBoard 5.1.2

**A smoother eraser, especially near PDFs.** While you rub, GazBoard no longer redraws a background copy of the whole screen between moves. With a PDF or document page in view, that meant redrawing the whole page dozens of times a second, which is what made the eraser drag there. The eraser's ring also moves on a layer of its own, straight from the pen or mouse, so it stays right under your hand, and a pen's eraser end gets the same ring. Rubbing notes off a document page no longer redraws the page's shadow on every move.

**Replay jumps land where you click.** Clicking further along the replay bar while it plays now goes exactly there, instead of stopping somewhat short.
