## What's new in GazBoard 5.0.1

**Very full boards no longer freeze the app.** A board with thousands upon thousands of strokes (one of ours was an ink-wash painting of 15,000) used to lock GazBoard up for seconds every time it opened, zoomed or settled after a pan, long enough for Android to offer to close the app. A board that full is now painted a few hundred strokes at a time: it fills in over a moment on opening, and while you zoom or pan you see the last finished picture until the sharp one is ready. Taps, pinches and Back are answered the whole time. Ordinary boards are drawn exactly as before.
