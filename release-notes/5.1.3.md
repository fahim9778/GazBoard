## What's new in GazBoard 5.1.3

**Search in Settings.** A search box at the top of Settings narrows the long list as you type, and lights up the words it found. Type a section name like "inking" to see that whole section.

**A pen glow for screen recordings.** Zoom and other recorders only show the mouse pointer, so pen writing appeared out of nowhere in recordings. Turn on "Show the pen in screen recordings" in Settings and a faint glow follows your pen, hovering or writing, so viewers can see where you are.

**Draw ahead of the pen.** A new setting, off by default, draws a short guess of where your pen is heading so the line keeps up with the nib. The guess is never saved: your ink is kept exactly as you wrote it.
