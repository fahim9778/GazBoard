## What's new in GazBoard 5.0.2

**Writing near the edges of the screen works properly now.** The board used to slide along under your pen whenever you wrote close to an edge, so words in the margin came out smeared. The pen, highlighter and eraser now leave the board still and put the ink exactly where you write, right up to the edge. Dragging a selection, a lasso or an object into the edge still scrolls the board, as before.

**No more ink out of sight.** A stroke carried past the edge of the screen, or over the toolbar or the page pictures, stops there instead of drawing where you can't see it. Bring the pen back on and a new stroke starts where it comes in.
