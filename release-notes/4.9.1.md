## What's Changed

**Pencil pointer:** with the pencil in hand the pointer is now a little pencil, matching the one in the tray, instead of a pen.

**Clearer colour picker:** the "any colour" button in the pen and pencil menus is now a colour wheel with your current ink in the middle, so it no longer looks like a second copy of the first swatch.

**Windows:** 4.9.1 also brings the pencil to Windows, whose 4.9.0 installer was held back by an over-strict test.
