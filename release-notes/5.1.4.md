## What's new in GazBoard 5.1.4

**GazBoard is on Google Play.** Android users can now install it from the Play Store and get updates there: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gazboard.app

**A lighter Android app.** The Play download leaves out pictures and text the app never uses, and the top and bottom bars are painted the way newer Android versions expect, so they keep matching your light or dark board.

**Getting ready for the Microsoft Store.** Each Windows release now also builds a Microsoft Store package. Copies installed from the Store will be kept up to date by the Store itself.
