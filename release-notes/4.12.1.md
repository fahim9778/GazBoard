## What's Changed

**Filling in small shapes with the pen no longer opens a menu.** Colouring in a note head or a small circle used to count as holding the pen still, so the board menu popped up and the ink vanished. GazBoard now tells a resting pen from a scribbling one. If you never want a held pen to open menus, turn off Settings → Hold the pen still for menus; the pen's side button and the selection bar still reach every menu.

**Type the protractor's angle.** The protractor's ⋯ menu has an Arm angle box: type 37 and the arm points at exactly 37°, with the outer scale's reading beside it.

**Small fixes from our Google Play testers.** On a phone, Present mode now says to tap ✕ on the bar instead of pressing Esc, and the backup buttons in My boards are the same width as the button above them.
