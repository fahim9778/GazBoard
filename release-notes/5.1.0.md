## What's new in GazBoard 5.1.0

**Neon pen.** A new pen effect that glows like a neon sign: a bright core with a soft halo in the ink's own colour. It looks best on a dark page, and still glows on a white one. Turn any pen neon from its menu (Effect → Neon); it comes with eight neon colours, purple first, and keeps the glow in PNG, PDF and SVG exports.

**The eraser rubs out shapes.** Circles, boxes, triangles, lines and arrows (drawn with the shape tool, or straightened from your ink) are now erased when the eraser crosses their outline, and undo brings them back. Rubbing out writing inside a box leaves the box alone, and boxes with text in them, locked shapes, pictures, notes and imported pages are never erased.

**Press and hold picks up your drawing, not the ring.** Holding a finger or pen in the gaps of a drawing, anywhere inside a group, or inside what is already selected now picks it up to move, at any zoom. The shortcut ring only opens on board with nothing close by.

**Lines that go where you draw them.** A line or arrow started on another line now starts exactly where you put it, instead of jumping towards that line's middle, and one drawn close to a line's end snaps neatly onto it, so corners meet and shapes close. Hold Alt (Option on a Mac) to draw a line with no snapping at all. Thanks to Thread-Reaper for reporting it (#47).

**Arrows side by side.** Two or more straight arrows between the same two boxes, in either direction, now sit next to each other instead of on top of one another.

**A bigger toolbar on a phone turned sideways.** In landscape there's room for every pen, the highlighter, select, text, sticky note, shapes and the ruler right on the bar. Turn the phone back and the compact bar returns, with your pen still in hand.

**Ink stops cleanly at the edge.** A stroke carried off the screen now ends exactly at the edge, and picks up again exactly where the pen comes back in.
