## What's new in GazBoard 5.2.1

**Stretch the ruler.** Drag the white ↔ dot near the ruler's right end to make it longer or shorter, so one ruler reaches right across a classroom smart board. The left end stays put, the blue knobs still turn it, and it comes back at the length you left it.

**Clearer ruler numbers.** When you zoom out, numbers that would sit under the ruler's buttons are left off, so nothing overlaps.

**Web version.** Offline setup no longer asks for a file it does not need.
