## What's new in GazBoard 5.2.0

**Handwriting to text.** Lasso some handwriting and choose Convert to text: it becomes a text box in the same place, in the same colour, with your line breaks kept. One Undo brings the ink back exactly as you drew it. Nothing ever converts on its own.

**Read on your own device.** On Windows it uses Windows' built-in handwriting recognizer. On Android it uses Google's on-device recognizer, which asks once before downloading a language pack (about 20 MB) and works offline after that. Your writing is never sent anywhere. Pick the language in Settings → Inking → Handwriting language.

**Now on the Microsoft Store.** GazBoard is on Google Play and the Microsoft Store, as well as GitHub and the web.
