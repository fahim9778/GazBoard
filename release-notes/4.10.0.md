## What's Changed

**Music staff and guitar tab paper:** two new patterns in Canvas. Staff paper has groups of five lines; tab paper has six, with T-A-B at the start. The spacing buttons set how big each staff is, and both print true to size.

**View mode:** press Ctrl+Alt+V (or More › View mode) to move around a board with no risk of changing it. The laser still points, and the highlighter's marks fade away by themselves and are never saved. Press Done or Ctrl+Alt+V again to edit; Esc does not leave it, so a stray key press can't switch editing back on.

**Page strip:** on a paper board, click the small tab on the left edge (or Show page pictures in the page navigator's ⋯ menu) to see every page down the side, like a PDF reader. Click a page to go there. On phones the strip is slimmer.

**Boards with many pictures open faster:** pictures and imported pages are fetched six at a time instead of one by one, which helps most with a boards folder in Google Drive or OneDrive.

**Behind the scenes:** the Google Play copy of the app never checks GitHub for updates; its store does that.
