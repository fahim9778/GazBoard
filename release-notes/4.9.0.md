## What's Changed

**Pencil:** a new pen in the tray (key 7) that draws grainy graphite. Press lightly to sketch and firmly to draw, go over a line to make it darker, and tip your stylus on its side to shade. Pick any colour for coloured pencils.

**Erase pencil marks:** sketch in pencil, ink over it, then clear just the pencil from the pencil's menu or the eraser's menu. Your ink stays, and undo brings the pencil back.

**Pencil sound:** a soft scratch while you draw with the pencil. Faster and firmer is louder, shading on its side is a softer hush, and holding still goes quiet. Turn it off in the pencil's menu or in Settings.
