## What's new in GazBoard 5.0.3

**Put the ruler away right where it is.** Every ruler, both set squares and the protractor now have a small ✕ beside their ⋯ button: one tap and it's gone. Hide ruler is also at the bottom of the ⋯ menu. The + menu still brings it back.

**The toolbar shows what's in your hand.** The ruler button lights up the moment you show the ruler. On phones, + lights up while a sticky note, text, shape, emoji or the ruler is in use, and ⋯ lights up for select, lasso, pan and the laser.

**Two hands on a big touch screen.** On smart boards and other large touchscreens, a resting hand, a sleeve or a second person touching the board no longer wipes out the stroke you're writing or zooms the board. Two fingers put down together still pinch-zoom, as on any phone or tablet.
