# GazBoard privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. This policy covers GazBoard on Android (from Google Play, F-Droid or GitHub), Windows, macOS, Linux and the web version. GazBoard is made by Md. Fakhruddin Gazzali Fahim (theBoringCode). Contact: fahim9778@gmail.com.

## The short version

GazBoard has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. Your boards are stored on your own device, in the folder you choose, and they leave it only when you export, save, share or send them yourself.

## What stays on your device

Everything you make in GazBoard: boards, pictures and documents you add, settings, and the list of recent boards. The Android app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so its private boards are not copied to Google Drive by the system. If you choose a boards folder that a sync service watches (for example Google Drive or Dropbox on a computer), that service copies the files under its own terms. GazBoard does not.

## When GazBoard uses the network

GazBoard works with no internet connection. It connects to anything only in these cases.

**Checking for updates (GitHub and desktop copies only).** On first launch GazBoard asks whether it may check for new versions. If you say yes, it asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the list of GazBoard releases from time to time, and desktop copies download an update from GitHub when you agree to install it. Like any website, GitHub sees the request, including your IP address and a line saying which GazBoard version is asking. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that request. You can say no, or turn this off later in Settings. Copies installed from Google Play or F-Droid never check GitHub. Their store updates them.

**Downloading a Chinese font (optional).** Chinese text needs a large font that is not built in. GazBoard downloads it only when you ask, from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) or GitHub. Those services see the download request.

**Sharing on your local network (optional, off by default).** When you turn on sharing, GazBoard finds other GazBoards on the same Wi-Fi and sends a board straight to the device you choose. Nothing goes through the internet or any server, including ours. A board arriving on your device is shown to you first and is kept only if you accept it. On Android, sharing runs as a visible notification while it is switched on.

**The web version.** The web version is a set of files served by a hosting provider. That provider sees the request when the page loads, as for any website. After that, your boards stay in your browser's own storage.

**Links you open.** If you open a web link from GazBoard (for example the GitHub page), your browser opens it under that site's own policy.

## Permissions on Android

GazBoard asks only for what these features need: internet (for the optional update check and font download), network and Wi-Fi state and multicast (to find other GazBoards on your Wi-Fi), a foreground service with notifications (to keep local sharing running while it is on), and access to the files and folders you pick yourself. It never asks for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone number.

## Children

GazBoard is a general teaching tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
